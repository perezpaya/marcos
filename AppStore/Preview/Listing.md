## Description

A warmer screen. One click away.

Marcos brings red, orange, and amber tints to your Mac. Choose a color, set the strength, and click the glasses in your menu bar to turn it on or off.

FIND YOUR KIND OF WARM
Go for a subtle glow or a deeper tint. Adjust the strength from 0 to 100% to suit what you are doing. Sliding to zero clears the tint; your next click restores your last strength.

ONE SETTING. EVERY DISPLAY.
Apply the same tint across your connected displays, including full-screen apps. Your chosen color and strength are saved between sessions.

READY WHEN YOU ARE
Keep Marcos in the menu bar and optionally launch it when you log in. Right-click or Control-click the glasses for color, strength, and login options. Colored lenses mean the tint is on. Clear lenses mean it is off.

YOUR MAC. YOUR SETTINGS.
Marcos works locally. No account, advertising, tracking, or analytics. Your tint settings stay on your Mac, and Marcos does not capture or inspect screen content.

Built for Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or later.
