# Marcos privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026.

Marcos applies a color tint to your Mac's displays. The app does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

## Local settings

Marcos stores the chosen tint color and strength on your Mac using macOS preferences. Launch-at-login registration is managed by macOS. These settings are used to provide the app's controls and restore your preferences.

Marcos reads the connected display layout to position its tint windows. It does not capture or inspect screen content.

## External links

Opening the privacy or support links takes you to GitHub in your browser. GitHub's privacy policy applies to your use of that website.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the [Marcos issue tracker](https://github.com/perezpaya/marcos/issues).
